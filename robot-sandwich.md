## Assumptions
- The Robot's hands are empty.
- The Bread, Peanut Butter, Plate, and Knife are on the table.
- The Bread has been unwrapped and the Peanut Butter jar is unscrewed, with the lid off.

## Steps
1. Pick up one slice of bread, ensure that it is laying flat on your hand.
2. Pick up the Knife.
3. Using the knife, scoop out one spoonful of butter.
4. Using the knife, spread the butter onto one side of the bread, use the side that is facing upward.
5. Place slice of bread onto the Plate, with the peanut butter side facing upward.
6. Pick up another slice of bread, ensure that it is laying flat on your hand.
7. Using the knife, scoop out one spoonful of butter.
8. Using the knife, spread the butter onto one side of the bread, use the side that is facing upward.
9. Place the peanut butter side of the newly buttered slice face-down on the existing slice, so that the peanut butter side of the newly buttered slice is touching the peanut butter side of the existing slice. Ensure the sandwich remains on the plate.
10. Place knife inside of peanut butter jar, with the blade end facing down.

## Done When
- Two slices of bread, each with peanut butter on them, are sitting on top of one another, with each of their peanut butter sides facing each other from top to bottom.
- The knife used to spread the peanut butter has been placed inside the peanut butter jar, with the blade end of the knife facing down in the jar.
