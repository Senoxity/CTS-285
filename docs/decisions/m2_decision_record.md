# M2 Elicitation Decision Record

## Investigation Path
1. What does “modern” need to mean from a user perspective?
   Evidence revealed: Stakeholders say modern means the experience should work reliably in a browser, be understandable without a printed manual, and avoid making the learner navigate unnecessary screens. They do not specify a visual style or framework.
2. Which original DataMan behaviors are considered essential to preserve?
   Evidence revealed: Stakeholders identify immediate answer feedback, repeated practice after an incorrect response, and a clear way for learners to see progress as central to the original experience.
3. What do parents or teachers need to understand about learner activity?
   Evidence revealed: Adults want to understand what the learner practiced and whether progress is occurring, but stakeholders have not yet agreed on a detailed reporting dashboard.

## Initial Position
**Supported evidence:**
Stakeholders want the team to create a new version of dataman that supports modern frameworks (particularly web browsers). They wish to maintain the basic structure of dataman, i.e. immediate answer feedback, repeated practice for incorrect answers, and the ability to view the user's progress. Stakeholders also wish to make it clear for Adults whether or not practice is occurring, and if the student is improving.

**Remaining uncertainty:**
Who will use the system, the visual style of the program, and what database technology is used are still unclear for the time being.

**Likely functional requirement:**
Entering a math problem into the revised dataman should return feedback to the user.

**Likely non-functional requirement / quality constraint:**
Using the program must be simple for the students, and intuitive for the adults.

**Assumption or proposed solution I am not treating as confirmed:**
It is assumed that the revised dataman should be compatible with all modern viewports (tablets, phones, laptops, etc.).

**Why my initial position is defensible:**
All of the facts represented in the initial position are supported by evidence from the requests of stakeholders.

## Complication
Students may use DataMan on school Chromebooks, phones, tablets, and home computers. Some sessions may be interrupted before intentional sign-out.

**What this affects:**
The new information affects how the team will tackle flexibility when developing dataman. The team now has to ensure that the program is still viewable on all of the different types of viewports users are expected to utilize.

**What I revised, if anything:**
Revising the initial position is not immediately necessary, since I accurately predicted this new constraint in the assumption section.

**Final decision and reasoning:**
Keeping the initial position will suffice for the time being. Unless another complication were to arise in the future, the initial position still holds up in the current situation.
